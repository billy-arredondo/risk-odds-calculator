Behind the numbers

All outcomes. No simulations.

Each die has six equally likely faces. We enumerate all 6A + D ordered outcomes for an unknown roll, or 6D defender outcomes when attacker dice are known. Sorted dice are compared in pairs, and ties go to the defender.

For a battle, each possible loss leads to a smaller battle. Dynamic programming memoizes these states and combines their probabilities until the defender has no armies or the attacker has only one. The origin’s final army is never risked or counted as a casualty.

These are exact combinatorial models evaluated with floating-point arithmetic, not arbitrary-precision fractions. Percentages are rounded to one decimal; complete distributions are rounded together to total 100%. A displayed 0.0% or 100.0% may be a rounded value. Army inputs are limited to {{ maxArmies }} per side to keep calculations responsive.
